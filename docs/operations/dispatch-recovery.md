# Recovering dispatch blocked by stale work ownership

Central can reconcile legacy ticket rows whose lifecycle proves there is no current work lease. The authoritative lifecycle is an open, terminal, or human-waiting ticket state; current work ownership is valid only for the pre-submission states. For inactive states Central checks work and review expiration evidence before recovery. A future, malformed, or contradictory lease expiration is ambiguous and remains protected for investigation rather than being cleared.

During its ordinary board transaction/reconciliation paths (dispatch reconciliation, offer creation, and `board_reap`), Central moves stale current-owner fields into `last_claimed_*` audit metadata, records `last_release_reason="inactive ticket lifecycle"`, removes only current work-lease fields, and appends one bounded `work_ownership_reconciled` dispatch-history record. No owner identity or historical attribution is discarded. The transaction uses the normal Central board lock/generation fence; it is not a direct SQLite or ticket-local repair.

An open ticket's existing eligible offer remains valid after reconciliation. A normal authenticated offered-seat claim establishes a fresh principal/agent lease and renews it; the prior owner has no inherited renewal or mutation authority. Repeated reaping after recovery is a no-op, so it does not continually revoke and recreate offers or grow history. Reconciliation is general across board, project, and ticket IDs; it has no live-ticket exception list.

For a persistently blocked ticket, inspect the Central ticket and dispatch history, status, work expiration fields, and review lease through supported Central reads. Allow the registered Butler's normal Central reconciliation/reaper path to repair a provably inactive row. Do not edit SQLite, blindly clear fields, manipulate offers, bypass claim gates, or reset a live work/review lease. If active or malformed expiration evidence is present, preserve the row and escalate it through the authorized operational channel for evidence-led review.
