## Current product and governance limitations

### One-owner local deployment boundary

**What you may encounter:** Pursers is not designed for a shared untrusted host,
a multi-person board, or remote public access.

**Current boundary:** Run Central, the dashboards, and agent seats for one owner
on one machine using loopback connections. This boundary is narrower than the
authentication and authorization model may appear to allow.

**Tracking:** `docs/QUALITY.md` and `docs/releases/v5.0.0b1.md`.

### Fleet CodeQL alert remains open on the current source

**What you may encounter:** Static analysis reports a
`py/polynomial-redos` warning in Fleet dashboard configuration redaction.

**Current boundary:** The finding is outside the six released wheels and the
AionUi extension ZIP, so it was not a Beta.1 artifact gate. A reviewed source
fix exists, but it is not part of the current Beta.2 working source and must not
be claimed until it lands.

**Tracking:** `TK-da393a0cdcb4`.

### Strict review does not yet enforce principal independence

**What you may encounter:** Two different seat names that share one principal
can satisfy the current seat-level self-review check.

**Current boundary:** Provision the reviewer under a different principal and
verify the submitting and reviewing principal IDs in the review record. The
repository audit found no historical misuse, but the current source does not
enforce that comparison itself.

**Tracking:** `TK-a2115c493a18`.

## First-run operational notes

### Match the Fleet dashboard URL scheme to Central

**What you may encounter:** The Fleet dashboard defaults to
`http://127.0.0.1:8766/mcp`. A TLS-enabled Central requires an `https://` URL;
using the default scheme cannot connect to it.

**Current boundary:** Pass the complete Central URL explicitly when starting
Fleet. Beta.2 source includes a clearer TLS mismatch diagnostic, but it cannot
choose the deployment's scheme for you.

**Tracking:** `TK-a4972ef8fab0`.

### Resume waits from the saved cursor

**What you may encounter:** Offers are time-limited. An offer seen in an older
notification may already be expired or may belong to another seat by the time
you act on it.

**Current boundary:** Resume the push wait from its saved positive cursor and
claim only a current, unexpired offer addressed to the active seat. Beta.2
source reconciles missed actionable offers from the bounded backlog; resetting
the cursor or treating an old notification as authority is still unsafe.

**Tracking:** `TK-c801f9c8a3d9` and `TK-939d37230fa3`.

### Beta.1 offer-deadline shutdown noise

**What you may encounter:** Beta.1 could log
`offer_deadline_error ... Cannot operate on a closed database` while Central
was shutting down.

**Current boundary:** The message did not indicate lost board data. Beta.2
source includes recovery from stale SQLite transaction contexts; this note is
retained so an operator comparing Beta.1 logs does not mistake old noise for a
new Beta.2 data-loss signal.

**Tracking:** `TK-62a2d109171b`.

## Resolved before the Beta.2 freeze

The following items appeared in earlier planning material but are not current
limitations at the source used for this draft:

- ACP P1 is present in the current tree, including its executed acceptance
  path. Tracking: `TK-79f5f9b84fed` and `TK-37bfac747069`.
- Central, Client, and Personal currently pin MCP Python SDK 2.2.0; the earlier
  evaluation merge was reverted, then a reviewed dependency ticket landed the
  upgrade. Tracking: `TK-1bdfc3931bd3` and `TK-9910ee9be69f`.
- The AionUi Connect helper now writes and consumes its actual loopback origin,
  so concurrent helpers are no longer forced to use the `43121` prefill.
  Tracking: `TK-6fa1eabbe4f3`.

Do not move a resolved item back into the limitations list unless the frozen
candidate removes its fix or new product evidence shows a regression.
