## Editorial guardrails

- Do not show Settings. It is not part of the public projection.
- Do not identify or count operators, seats, principals, repositories, boards,
  hosts, or customers from the aliases or empty states.
- Do not describe `none` as a verified private zero.
- Do not call the active-card sequence faster, cheaper, or more productive than
  a sequential run; no matched baseline is present in this source run.
- Do not add browser chrome, terminal output, source JSON, metadata overlays,
  notifications, cursor trails, or a public listener.
- If the source is recaptured, regenerate all six frames and `manifest.json`
  from one projection so health, freshness, aliases, and buckets stay coherent.
