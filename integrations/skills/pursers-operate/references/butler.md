# Autonomous Butler responsibilities

Treat Butler proposals, desired state, approvals, reconciliation, and delivery
as separate audited stages. Respect revision fences, capacity and budget limits,
cooldowns, active leases, project boundaries, and configured delivery mode.
Never turn unsupported capability into a healthy empty result.
